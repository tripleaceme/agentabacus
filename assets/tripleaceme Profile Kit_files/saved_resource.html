Questions that find the numbers you haven't written down

For every bullet that currently has no number, one of these usually produces one. Answer them out loud and the bullet writes itself.

  • Scale — how many models, tables, pipelines, sources, dashboards or users did this touch? "Across 140 dbt models" is a number.
  • Time — what took hours before and minutes after? What ran nightly and now runs hourly? How long did the migration take end to end?
  • Money — warehouse spend, licence spend, contractor hours avoided, headcount not hired. Even a defensible estimate beats nothing.
  • Failure rate — how many incidents, failed runs, or bad-data escalations per month before, and after?
  • Reach — how many people or teams use what you built? Did adoption spread beyond the team that asked for it?
  • Firsts — were you the first to do this here? Did it become the standard others now follow? "Established the team's first…" carries seniority.
  • Blast radius — what would have broken if you hadn't done it? That's the value, stated in reverse.
Two structural rules for the Experience section

Cap each role at three to five bullets — a wall of eight gets skimmed to zero. And front-load the verb: Migrated, Built, Cut, Established, Automated, Shipped. Never open a bullet with "Responsible for" or "Worked on" — both describe a job description rather than a person.
